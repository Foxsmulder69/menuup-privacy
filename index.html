Privacy Policy

Menu Up — a digital menu-board app made by Austech.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

We do not collect your personal data

Menu Up does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

  • The app runs entirely on your own device and local Wi-Fi network.
  • Menus are created on a Menu Up Controller companion app on your phone or tablet (iPhone, iPad or Android) and sent directly to your TV over your own local network — never to us or any third party. Photos you add to a menu are chosen by you from your device and stay on your devices and your TV only. This policy covers both the Menu Up TV app and the Menu Up Controller app.
  • Your menu content is stored only on the TV itself, and the app works fully offline.

Permissions

The app uses your local network only to receive menus from your phone or iPad on the same Wi-Fi. It does not use the internet to transmit any personal data.

Free trial and subscriptions

Menu Up is free for 7 days, then needs a monthly or yearly subscription. Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your name, email or payment details.

So that deleting and reinstalling the Android app does not restart the free trial, the Android app sends our server a one-way code made from the phone's app-specific Android ID (the ID itself is never sent and cannot be worked out from the code), together with the date the trial started. That code and date are the only things stored. They are not linked to you or to any account, are not shared with anyone, and are used for nothing else. On iPhone and iPad the trial date stays on the device.

Children

Menu Up is a business tool intended for use by businesses and is not directed at children.

Third parties

We do not share any data with third parties.
